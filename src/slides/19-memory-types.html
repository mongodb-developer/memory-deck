4 types of long-term memory

Episodictype: "episodic"Things that happened:

“Yesterday I rated matcha with 1 star.”

Semantictype: "semantic"Facts and preferences:

“I’m allergic to gluten.” (direct)
“Prefers oat milk.” (generalized)

Proceduraltype: "procedural"How to do a task:

“Deliver on Saturday mornings, never on weekdays.”

Taxonomictype: "taxonomic"What terms mean:

“‘The usual order’ means bread, eggs and bananas.”

Short-term memory is the current conversation. “Lemons tonight”, “I bought a blender” remain there.
One example per type, all from the grocery agent. Short-term memory is just the conversation itself.
